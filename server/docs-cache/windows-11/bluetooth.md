# Pair a Bluetooth device on Windows 11

Bluetooth lets the computer talk to wireless headphones, speakers,
mice, keyboards, and phones — over the air, without cables.

## Turning Bluetooth on

1. Open **Settings → Bluetooth & devices**.
2. Make sure the **Bluetooth** switch at the top is set to **On**.

You can also click the network/sound area in the bottom-right corner of
the screen and click the **Bluetooth** tile to toggle it.

## Pairing a new device

1. Put the device into pairing mode. For most headphones and speakers
   this means holding the power button until a light flashes.
2. In **Settings → Bluetooth & devices**, click **Add device**.
3. Choose **Bluetooth** at the top of the dialog.
4. Wait a few seconds. When the device appears in the list, click it.
5. If a passcode shows, confirm it on the device.

## Removing a paired device

In **Settings → Bluetooth & devices**, find the device card. Click the
three dots and choose **Remove device**.

## When pairing fails

* Make sure the device's battery is charged.
* Move the device closer to the computer (within 3 feet).
* Turn Bluetooth off, wait 10 seconds, turn it back on.
* If the device was paired to another computer or phone, it may need
  to be cleared on that other device first.
