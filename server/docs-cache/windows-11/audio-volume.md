# Sound and volume on Windows 11

## Adjusting volume

* Click the **speaker icon** in the bottom-right corner near the clock.
  A slider appears — drag it left to lower, right to raise.
* On most laptops, dedicated volume keys are along the top row of the
  keyboard (look for small speaker icons). Hold **Fn** if needed.

## Choosing where sound comes out

If you have headphones or external speakers connected:

1. Click the **speaker icon** in the bottom-right corner.
2. Click the **arrow** next to the volume slider.
3. Pick the audio device you want — for example "Headphones" or
   "External Speakers".

## When there is no sound

1. Make sure the speaker icon is not muted.
2. Verify the right output device is selected (see above).
3. Open **Settings → System → Sound** and click **Troubleshoot common
   sound problems**.
4. As a last resort, restart the computer.

## Headphone jack not working

* Plug the headphones in firmly — the jack often needs an extra push.
* Try a different pair of headphones to rule out the headphones
  themselves.
* In **Settings → System → Sound**, check that the right output is
  selected.
