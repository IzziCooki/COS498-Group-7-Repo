# Add a printer on Windows 11

## USB printer

1. Plug the printer into the computer with its USB cable.
2. Turn the printer on.
3. Windows usually detects it automatically and finishes setup in the
   background — a notification will say "your device is set up".

If nothing happens after one minute:

1. Open **Settings → Bluetooth & devices → Printers & scanners**.
2. Click **Add device**.
3. Wait for the printer to appear, then click **Add device** next to
   its name.

## Wi-Fi or network printer

1. Make sure the printer is on the same Wi-Fi network as the computer.
2. Open **Settings → Bluetooth & devices → Printers & scanners**.
3. Click **Add device**.
4. The printer should appear in the list within 30 seconds. Click
   **Add device** next to it.

If the printer does not appear, click **Add manually** at the bottom
and follow the prompts. You will need either the printer's IP address
or its model name.

## Setting a default printer

1. **Settings → Bluetooth & devices → Printers & scanners**.
2. Turn off **Let Windows manage my default printer**.
3. Click the printer you want as default and choose **Set as default**.

## Clearing a stuck print job

1. **Settings → Bluetooth & devices → Printers & scanners**.
2. Click the printer.
3. Click **Open print queue**.
4. Right-click the stuck job and choose **Cancel**.
