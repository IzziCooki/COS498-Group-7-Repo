# Notifications and Focus on Windows 11

Notifications appear in the bottom-right corner of the screen. They go
into the **Notification Center**, which you open by clicking the date
and time at the bottom-right.

## Turning notifications on or off (everywhere)

1. Open **Settings → System → Notifications**.
2. Toggle **Notifications** at the top.

## Per-app notification control

In the same Notifications screen, scroll to **Notifications from apps
and other senders**. Each app has its own switch. Turn off the apps
you do not want notifying you.

## Quiet hours with Focus

Focus is Windows 11's "do not disturb" mode. While Focus is on,
notifications are silenced and saved for later.

1. Open **Settings → System → Focus**.
2. Set a duration (default 30 minutes).
3. Click **Start focus session**.

You can also turn Focus on instantly from the Notification Center
clock by clicking **Do not disturb**.

## Schedule automatic Focus

In the Focus settings:

* **Turn on do not disturb automatically** — set rules like "during
  these hours" or "when sharing my screen".
* **Show notifications on the lock screen** — turn off if you share
  the computer or want privacy.
