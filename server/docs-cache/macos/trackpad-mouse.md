# Trackpad and mouse on Mac

## Trackpad gestures

* **Tap** — light tap to click (if **Tap to click** is enabled).
* **Two-finger scroll** — scroll up and down.
* **Pinch** — zoom in and out (Photos, Safari, PDF).
* **Two-finger swipe between pages** — back/forward in Safari.
* **Three-finger swipe up** — Mission Control.
* **Three-finger swipe down** — App Exposé (windows of the current app).
* **Three-finger swipe left/right** — switch full-screen apps and
  desktops.
* **Pinch with thumb and three fingers** — Launchpad.
* **Spread thumb and three fingers** — show desktop.

Configure all of these in **System Settings → Trackpad**.

## Pointer speed

Drag the **Tracking speed** slider in **System Settings → Trackpad
→ Point & Click**.

## Right-click on a Mac

* **Two-finger tap** on the trackpad, or
* **Control + click** with one finger, or
* On a Magic Mouse: tap the right side (enable **Secondary click** in
  **System Settings → Mouse**).

## Natural scrolling

Macs default to **natural scrolling** — the page moves with your
fingers, like a touchscreen. If you prefer the older direction:

1. **System Settings → Trackpad → Scroll & Zoom**.
2. Turn off **Natural scrolling**.

## When the trackpad jumps around

Crumbs or moisture can make the trackpad register false touches.
Wipe the trackpad with a slightly damp microfiber cloth and dry
hands. If the cursor still misbehaves, restart the Mac.
