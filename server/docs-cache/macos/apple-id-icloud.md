# Apple ID and iCloud on Mac

Your Apple ID is the email address you use for the App Store, iCloud,
iMessage, and FaceTime. iCloud is Apple's cloud storage that syncs
between Mac, iPhone, and iPad.

## Signing in to your Apple ID

1. Open **System Settings**.
2. Click **Sign in** at the top of the sidebar.
3. Enter your Apple ID email and password.
4. Approve the sign-in on another Apple device if prompted.

## What iCloud syncs

In **System Settings → Apple ID → iCloud**, choose what to sync:

* **iCloud Drive** — files in Documents and Desktop folders.
* **Photos** — your photo library.
* **Mail**, **Contacts**, **Calendars**, **Reminders**, **Notes**.
* **Safari** — bookmarks, tabs, passwords.
* **iCloud Keychain** — saved passwords across devices.
* **Find My Mac** — locate the Mac if it is lost.

## Free vs paid iCloud storage

Every Apple ID gets 5 GB of iCloud free. Paid plans:

* **iCloud+ 50 GB** — small monthly fee.
* **iCloud+ 200 GB** — common family plan.
* **iCloud+ 2 TB** — heavy use.

## Two-factor authentication

When you sign in to a new device, Apple sends a 6-digit code to your
trusted devices. Always enable two-factor authentication.

## Signing out

In **System Settings → Apple ID**, scroll to the bottom and click
**Sign Out**. macOS asks whether to keep a copy of iCloud files
locally — say yes if you want to keep editing offline.
