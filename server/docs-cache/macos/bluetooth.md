# Pair a Bluetooth device on Mac

## Turning Bluetooth on

* Click the **Control Center** icon (two pills) in the menu bar, then
  click the **Bluetooth** tile, or
* Open **System Settings → Bluetooth** and turn the switch on.

## Pairing a new device

1. Put the device into pairing mode. Most headphones have a button
   you hold for 3-5 seconds until a light flashes.
2. Open **System Settings → Bluetooth**.
3. Wait for the device to appear under **Nearby Devices**.
4. Click **Connect** next to it.
5. If a passcode shows, confirm it on the device.

## Reconnecting a known device

Once paired, the Mac reconnects automatically when the device is on
and in range. Some devices (especially headphones) need to be
manually selected from the Bluetooth menu when first turned on.

## Removing a paired device

1. **System Settings → Bluetooth**.
2. Hover over the device under **My Devices**.
3. Click the **i** (info) icon and choose **Forget This Device**.

## When pairing fails

* Charge the device.
* Move it within 3 feet of the Mac.
* If the device was previously paired with another Mac, iPad, or
  iPhone, unpair it on that other device first.

## AirPods special behavior

AirPods that have already been linked to your iCloud account pair
automatically when opened near the Mac. Click the volume icon in the
menu bar to choose them as audio output.
