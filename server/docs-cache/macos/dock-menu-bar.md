# The Dock and menu bar on Mac

The **Dock** is the row of icons at the bottom of the screen. The
**menu bar** is the strip at the top.

## Adding an app to the Dock

* Drag the app's icon from **Applications** onto the Dock, or
* Right-click a running app's Dock icon and choose **Options → Keep
  in Dock**.

## Removing an app from the Dock

Drag the icon away from the Dock until you see **Remove** appear, then
release. (The app itself is not deleted — only the Dock shortcut.)

## Moving the Dock to the side

The Dock can sit at the bottom, left, or right.

1. Open **System Settings → Desktop & Dock**.
2. Find **Position on screen** and choose **Left**, **Bottom**, or
   **Right**.

## Hiding the Dock

In the same panel, turn on **Automatically hide and show the Dock**.
The Dock disappears until you move the mouse to its edge of the
screen.

## The menu bar

Apple icon, app menus, status icons (Wi-Fi, battery, Control Center,
clock) — all live in the menu bar at the top of the screen.

## Adding or removing menu bar icons

1. **System Settings → Control Center**.
2. For each item, choose **Show in Menu Bar**, **Don't Show in Menu
   Bar**, or only in Control Center.
