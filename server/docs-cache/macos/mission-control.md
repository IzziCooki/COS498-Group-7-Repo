# Mission Control and Spaces on Mac

Mission Control is the bird's-eye view of every open window. Spaces
are macOS's virtual desktops.

## Opening Mission Control

* Three-finger swipe up on the trackpad, or
* Press the **Mission Control** key on the keyboard (looks like
  three rectangles), or
* Press **Control + Up Arrow**.

## Adding a new Space

In Mission Control, hover the top-right corner. A **+** button
appears. Click it to create a new desktop space.

## Switching Spaces

* Three-finger swipe left or right on the trackpad, or
* Press **Control + Left/Right Arrow**, or
* Open Mission Control and click a space at the top.

## Moving a window to another Space

* In Mission Control, drag a window's preview onto a space at the top.
* Or drag the window itself to the edge of the screen and hold —
  after a moment, the next Space slides in.

## Assigning an app to a specific Space

1. Right-click the app's icon in the Dock.
2. Choose **Options**.
3. Pick **All Desktops**, **This Desktop**, or **None**.

## Hot Corners

Hot Corners trigger Mission Control (or other actions) when you
move the mouse to a corner.

1. **System Settings → Desktop & Dock**.
2. Scroll to **Hot Corners…**.
3. Pick an action for each corner — Mission Control, Notification
   Center, Lock Screen, Sleep Display.
