# Sound and audio output on Mac

## Adjusting volume

* Use the **F11** and **F12** keys (volume down / volume up). On some
  Macs you must hold **Fn**.
* Or click the **volume icon** in the menu bar and drag the slider.

If the volume icon is missing, open **System Settings → Control
Center**, find **Sound**, and pick **Show in Menu Bar**.

## Choosing audio output

1. Click the **volume icon** in the menu bar.
2. Under **Output**, click the device you want — Mac speakers,
   headphones, AirPods, or external speakers.

Or open **System Settings → Sound → Output** for the full list.

## When there is no sound

* Make sure the volume is not at zero and not muted.
* In **System Settings → Sound → Output**, verify the right device is
  selected.
* Plug headphones in and out — sometimes the system gets stuck on the
  wrong output.
* Restart the Mac.

If sound still does not work, reset the audio system by quitting
**coreaudiod** in **Activity Monitor** — macOS restarts it
automatically.

## Microphone not working in calls

* **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone**: make sure
  the app has permission.
* **System Settings → Sound → Input**: pick the correct microphone
  and speak — the level meter should bounce.
