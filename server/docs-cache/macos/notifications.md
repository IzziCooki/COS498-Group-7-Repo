# Notifications and Focus on Mac

Notifications appear at the top-right corner of the screen and stack
in **Notification Center**, opened by clicking the date and time in
the menu bar.

## Per-app notification control

1. Open **System Settings → Notifications**.
2. Click an app in the list.
3. Toggle **Allow notifications**.
4. Choose **Banner** (auto-disappear) or **Alert** (stays until
   dismissed).
5. Toggle **Show on Lock Screen** if you want privacy.

## Turn off notification sounds

Per app: **System Settings → Notifications**, click the app, turn off
**Play sound for notifications**.

## Focus modes

Focus is macOS's "do not disturb" with personalities. Modes you can
turn on include:

* **Do Not Disturb** — silence everything.
* **Personal** — allow only personal contacts.
* **Work** — allow work apps and people.
* **Sleep** — silent during scheduled sleep hours.

## Turning Focus on or off

* Click **Control Center** in the menu bar, click **Focus**, pick a
  mode.
* Or set up automatic schedules in **System Settings → Focus**.

## Sharing Focus across devices

In **System Settings → Focus → Share across devices**, turn it on so
turning Focus on at your iPhone also silences your Mac (and vice
versa).
