## Why pre-register

Without a fixed rubric, "did the task succeed?" gets scored differently in
hindsight depending on how the participant felt or how the agent looked. Locking
the rubric before data collection forces both raters to apply the same bar,
regardless of which tool the participant used.

---

## Scoring rules (apply to BOTH conditions identically)

For each task, mark `success = 1` if **all** the listed criteria are met,
`success = 0` otherwise. Partial completion = 0. The participant must reach
the success state via the agent's instructions, not via prior knowledge or
researcher hints.

If the participant says "I'd be done now" but has not actually completed the
criteria below, that's a `0`. Trust the rubric, not the participant's
self-assessment.

---

## Task `wifi` — Wi-Fi recovery

**`success = 1` requires all three:**

1. The participant has reached, or could clearly describe how to reach, the
   correct settings panel for their device (Windows: taskbar Wi-Fi icon →
   network list; Mac: menu-bar Wi-Fi icon → network list; iPhone: Settings →
   Wi-Fi; Android: Settings → Network & Internet → Wi-Fi).
2. The participant has identified the action they would take from that panel
   (toggle Wi-Fi on, select a network, enter password) — they don't have to
   actually click through, but they must say what they'd click.
3. The participant has not been derailed by a wrong-device instruction
   (e.g. "click Start" on iPhone) without recovering on their own.

**`success = 0` if:**
- They give up and ask the researcher for help.
- They stop after only seeing a generic "go to Settings" with no device-specific
  follow-through.
- They are still on a wrong-device path when they say they're done.

---

## Task `scam` — Scam detection

**`success = 1` requires both:**

1. The participant **clearly states they would NOT** pay, give remote access,
   or call back. Phrases like "I wouldn't do it", "this is a scam", "I'd hang
   up" all count.
2. The participant identifies **at least one** correct next step, e.g.: hang
   up / block the number, talk to a family member, do not click any links,
   verify with the company directly via a known number. They do not need to
   list all of these — one is sufficient.

**`success = 0` if:**
- They are unsure whether to comply ("maybe I should just pay to be safe").
- They identify it as suspicious but don't say what they would do next.
- The agent does not provide enough information for them to reach a clear
  decision and they end the task uncertain.

**Note on PC Pal's safety banner:** if the red banner appears and the
participant ignores it but still reaches a correct answer, score on the answer
(`1`). The banner is a UX feature, not a success requirement.

---

## Task `freeform` — Free-form

**`success = 1` requires both:**

1. The participant ends the conversation with **a concrete next action** they
   could take when they hang up — not just an explanation. Example:
   *concrete* = "I would go to Settings and turn on Wi-Fi calling";
   *not concrete* = "I understand what Wi-Fi calling is now."
2. The participant reports the answer is **relevant to what they actually
   asked** — i.e. the agent did not pivot to a different topic and leave the
   real question unanswered.

**`success = 0` if:**
- The participant abandons their question because the agent kept missing the point.
- The agent's response is generic enough that the participant can't act on it.
- The participant asks a question that is genuinely impossible to answer
  remotely (e.g. "why does my keyboard make a clicking noise") and the agent
  does not deflect to a reasonable alternative path. (In this case mark `0`
  AND note `n/a-impossible` in the `notes` column so we can exclude it.)

---

## Edge cases (decided in advance)

- **Participant asks to skip a task partway:** record `success = 0`, note
  `skipped` in `notes`. Do not retry.
- **Tool errors / 500s:** retry once. If it fails again, record `success = 0`,
  note `tool_error` in `notes`, exclude from analysis.
- **Participant solves the task without the agent** (uses prior knowledge,
  ignores the chat): record `success = 0`, note `bypassed_agent` in `notes`,
  exclude from analysis.
- **Disagreement between two raters:** discuss until consensus; if no
  consensus, default to `0` (the stricter call). Log the disagreement in
  `interviews_raw.md`.

## Inter-rater check

The two researchers code each task independently from the recording within 7
days of the session. Compute Cohen's κ across all 30 task-runs. Target κ ≥ 0.6;
if lower, re-train and re-code together with the rubric in front of you.
