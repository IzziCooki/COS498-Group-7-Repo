## What this is

We are college students building a chatbot called **PC Pal** that helps people with computer and phone questions. Today we'd like you to try it out and tell us what you think. The whole session will take about **30–45 minutes**.

This is a **class project**, not a medical or psychological study. We are not testing you — we are testing our chatbot.

## What you'll do

1. Answer a few short questions about your background.
2. Try **three short tasks** twice — once with PC Pal, once with another chatbot (ChatGPT) — so we can compare them.
3. Tell us how each one went on a short survey (5 quick questions, takes ~1 minute each time).
4. Have a 5-minute chat with us at the end about what was helpful and what was confusing.

You can do everything from your own computer or phone over Zoom. We will share our screen if needed; you do **not** have to share yours unless you want to.

## What we'll record

- The Zoom call (audio + video) so we can review it later. We delete the recording within 30 days of the session.
- Your typed conversations with PC Pal and ChatGPT.
- Your answers on the surveys.
- Notes we take during the session.

We will give you a **participant code** (like `P03`). Your name will not appear in any write-up. Quotes we share in our class report will use that code only.

## Your rights

- This is **completely voluntary**. You can stop at any time, skip any question, or end the session for any reason — no explanation needed.
- If you change your mind after the session, email us within 7 days and we will delete your data.
- You will receive a **$15 gift card** as a thank-you whether or not you complete every task.

## What we won't do

- We will not ask you to type passwords, credit cards, or any private information.
- We will not install anything on your computer.
- We will not share your data outside of our class team and instructor.

## How to confirm

Please reply to the email with **"Yes, I agree"**, or say it on the Zoom call before we start. That's all we need.

Thank you so much for helping us — your honest reactions are exactly what we need.
